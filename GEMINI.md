3. **No Slop**: Zero corporate LinkedIn clichés, generic motivational aphorisms, engagement bait, or manufactured drama.
4. **Traceable Grounding**: All claims must resolve to `knowledge/` records, commit hashes, hardware specs, or user-confirmed facts.
